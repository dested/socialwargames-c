The roster — four pieces, drawn in code

Sketch style decodes to four rules: chunky silhouettes, 2–3 flat value planes lit from upper-left, a thick outline in a darkened cut of the fill, and soft contact shadows. All four rules are trivially procedural — which means infinite faction colors, damage states, and animation without ever opening an art tool.

Why this direction wins

The board is already built

fabletest-sketch's engine — infinite deterministic terrain, rivers, settlements, calibrated orientation tables — is the hardest asset, and it's done.

Pieces are the fiction

You don't command soldiers — you're a voice at a war table voting on moves. Board-game miniatures don't fight the theme; they are the theme.

Code owns the style

Procedural units mean faction palettes, veterancy marks, HP pips, and rally pins all speak one language — and nobody ever waits on an artist.
